Rules of thumb

  • One gift per character per week.
  • The first gift to someone can be anything; it unlocks meals. Use something cheap from the Lowtown tavern.
  • On a character's birthday, almost any gift gets "really liked". Unrecruited characters won't prompt you, so check the Calendar.
  • Grab the Gift Discount Boon of Salvation early.
  • Dagsion shops restock at the start of each chapter.

Best all-rounders

  • Tales of Adventure and Strategy Manuscript are favorites for a huge share of the cast.
  • Eastern Tea Leaves (Callianeira Port) is really liked by almost everyone.
  • Volcano and Crimson Ghosh cover most drinkers.
  • Switch to the Items tab for the full ranking.

Gift data: Game8, NightlyGamingBinge, Polygon, GameSpot, Selphie1999Gaming, KeenGamer and player reports. When guides disagree, an item counts as a favorite if any guide tested it as one, and conflicts are noted on the card. "Also good" lists whole categories that earn smaller gains.

Paralogue dates: KeenGamer, Pocket Tactics and GameTyrant.
